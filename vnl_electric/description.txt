Volvo VNL Electric concept

- Body, cabins, interiors and all 27 VNL frames (4x2, 6x2, 6x4) built on the 2025 Volvo VNL.
- Battery packs from the Volvo VNR Electric, sized to each frame in Standard, ER and XR range.
- An approximation of the Volvo e-axle is used as motors - 460 kW per driven axle, dual e-axle 920 kW on 6x4 frames. This creates space for a 3rd battery pack within the frame.
- VNL Electric factory paint job with an optional new logo on the sleeper cabs.

Requires the Volvo VNL (2025) DLC and Ownable Volvo VNR Electric mod.

Source and build instructions: https://github.com/barnstee/ATS-Mods

Please create issues at https://github.com/barnstee/ATS-Mods/issues or even better pull requests with the fixes if you find any bugs or have suggestions for improvements.

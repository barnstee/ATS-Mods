Fictional Volvo VNL Electric concept, built on the Volvo VNL (2025).

- Body, cabins, interiors and all 27 VNL frames (4x2, 6x2, 6x4) - battery packs from the Volvo VNR Electric, sized to each frame in Standard, ER and XR range, step ladder and exhaust removed.
- Only the electric drivetrain (2-speed transmission, e-motor sound) is taken from the VNR Electric.
- An approximation of the new Volvo e-axle is used as motors - 460 kW per driven axle, dual e-axle 920 kW on 6x4 frames.
- VNL Electric factory paint job with an optional new logo on the sleeper cabs.

Requires the Volvo VNL (2025) DLC and Ownable Volvo VNR Electric mod.

Source and build instructions: https://github.com/barnstee/ATS-Mods
